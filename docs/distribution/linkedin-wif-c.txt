"How do you audit CI access to GCP?"

If the answer is "we have a service account key and we know which teams have it", you have a finding waiting to happen.

WIF changes the answer to:

→ repo, branch, workflow, and actor captured on every call
→ queryable in Cloud Audit Logs via the federated principal / service-account delegation
→ revocable by deleting one IAM binding — and existing tokens age out quickly

Full production pattern — Terraform, failure modes, audit query, revocation playbook, Well-Architected mapping: https://gcp-architect-blog.web.app/posts/security-iam/001-workload-identity-federation-github-to-gcp/
